# CEFR Wordlist Sources

Both files here are redistributed from the [Open Language Profiles](https://github.com/openlanguageprofiles/olp-en-cefrj) repository.

- `cefrj-vocabulary-profile-1.5.csv` — CEFR-J Vocabulary Profile (ver 1.5), compiled by Yukio Tono, Tokyo University of Foreign Studies (the CEFR-J project). Free for research and commercial use with citation; copyright belongs to Tono Laboratory at TUFS.
- `octanove-vocabulary-profile-c1c2-1.0.csv` — Octanove Vocabulary Profile C1/C2 (ver 1.0), created by Octanove Labs. Licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

Reference: The CEFR-J Wordlist Version 1.5. Retrieved from http://www.cefr-j.org/download.html.
